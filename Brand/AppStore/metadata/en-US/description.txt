A quiet place to write. A beautiful page to share.

LeftBlank brings focused writing and live typesetting together on your Mac. Draft an essay, work through a technical idea, or build a longer manuscript. See the finished pages beside your words, then export a PDF when you are ready.

WRITE WITH ROOM TO THINK
A spacious editor, light and dark appearances, and a navigable outline help you stay with your writing. Autosave keeps your draft, while document history lets you compare and restore earlier versions.

EXPLAIN MORE THAN WORDS
Keep prose, equations, code blocks, tables, images, footnotes, and cross-references in one document. Write lecture notes that show the reasoning, or a report that makes the details easy to follow. Use Typst to lay out charts, vector diagrams, 16:9 presentation pages, and posters, then share them as PDFs.

SEE THE PAGE TAKE SHAPE
Switch between focused writing, side-by-side preview, and a full-page reading view. Check the actual typeset result as you work and export your document as a PDF.

FIND THE NEXT TOOL
Press Command-J to discover commands and insert common document elements. Browse templates and packages when your writing needs a different starting point.

KEEP YOUR SOURCE
LeftBlank edits Typst, a text-based typesetting language. Your .typ source stays editable and can be taken to other Typst tools. Common actions are available through commands; advanced layouts can use direct source editing.

Made for Mac with Apple silicon. Requires macOS 14 or later. English and Simplified Chinese interfaces are included. Core writing, bundled examples, preview, and PDF export work offline; downloading additional templates or packages requires an internet connection.
